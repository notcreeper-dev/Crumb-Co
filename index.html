A family kitchen that grew into a bakery

Crumb & Co. started in 2016, when Maya Ellis began baking loaves for neighbors on Saturday mornings. Word spread, and in 2018 she opened the shop on Market Street.

We still bake in small batches, buy flour and fruit from Oregon suppliers, and know many of our regulars by name. Anything left at closing goes to a local food pantry.

Why people choose Crumb & Co.

  • Freshly baked

    Everything is baked in the morning and sold the same day.

  • Quality ingredients

    Real butter, local flour and seasonal fruit. No shortcuts.

  • Local business

    Independent and owner-run, in the same neighborhood since 2018.

  • Custom orders

    Birthday cakes, wedding desserts and party boxes. Order 3 days ahead.
